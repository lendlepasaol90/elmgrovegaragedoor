## Elm Grove facts you may use (verified)
- Village of Elm Grove, Waukesha County, Wisconsin. ZIP 53122, area code 262. About 3.3 square miles; 2020 population about 6,500.
- Incorporated as a village in 1955 (partly to avoid annexation). Rapid home building after World War II; population just under 5,000 by 1960.
- Name traditionally credited to the groves of trees around the early settlement. Tree City USA community since 1989; mature trees and leafy, large-lot neighborhoods.
- Settled from the 1830s, many early German settlers. Watertown Plank Road (built 1848 with white oak planks, Milwaukee to Watertown) is still the village's main street and retail corridor. Railroad came in the 1850s; a depot was built in 1864.
- School Sisters of Notre Dame have had a campus in Elm Grove since 1855.
- Sunset Playhouse, a community theater founded in 1960, is at 800 Elm Grove Road.
- Elm Grove Village Park on Juneau Boulevard: about 78 acres with two ponds, walking trails, a bridge, playground, pool and sports fields.
- Underwood Creek runs through the village.
- Boundaries/roads: Bluemound Road (US 18) to the south area, North Avenue to the north, Pilgrim Parkway on the west, 124th Street on the east; Juneau Blvd, Elm Grove Road, Watertown Plank Road. I-94 and Hwy 45 nearby.
- Elmbrook School District (Tonawanda and Dixon elementary, Pilgrim Park Middle School). Elm Grove Public Library.
- The City of Brookfield surrounds much of the village.
- Housing mix: 1950s-1970s ranches, split-levels and Colonials on large wooded lots, older homes near the village center, some newer and rebuilt custom homes, detached and attached two- and three-car garages, long driveways shaded by mature trees.
- Climate: cold, snowy Wisconsin winters (subzero nights most winters; Milwaukee-area snowfall averages around 45 inches), road salt, freeze-thaw cycles, humid summers, heavy fall leaf drop that clogs bottom seals and tracks, tree sap and shade that keep doors damp.
- Nearby communities served: Elm Grove, Brookfield, Wauwatosa, West Allis, New Berlin, Menomonee Falls, Butler, Pewaukee, Waukesha, Milwaukee's west side, Greenfield, Hales Corners.
